My focus is business direction, process standardization, and turning practical needs into clear project requirements. I am learning to apply AI and automation to everyday business workflows.

### What I'm exploring

- **Product customization:** Turning material, color, sizing, and construction choices into structured requirements for human review.
- **Business workflows:** Connecting inquiries, sample approvals, production information, and shipping documents.
- **Product content:** Exploring AI-assisted visual workflows that preserve garment structure, details, and consistency.
- **WordPress:** Presenting customization capabilities and collecting B2B inquiries.
